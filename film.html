The launch film

One session, start to finish.

A 90-second product film. It follows a single 45-minute study session, and each tool appears at the moment it would really appear. Every screen in it is drawn from the app’s real layouts, colours and copy.

Visual identity

Indigo (#4F46E5) for focus, orange (#C2410C) for blocking, teal (#0F766E) for ScrollStop and breaks, red only for time’s up. The app’s Inter typeface. The background of the closing scenes follows the Home card’s morning, afternoon and evening gradients.

Motion

The app’s own curves: the Material “emphasized” ease for every move, the logo intro rebuilt from the app’s launch animation (overshoot 1.6, hand sweeping 200°), springy pops for success. Four transitions only: shape morphs, colour washes, a continuous camera, and session-clock jumps.

Sound

Room tone and stacking notifications, a hard cut to silence, then a 96 BPM pulse that stands for the timer, with pads and a four-note piano motif. Each chapter adds a layer; the break drops back to pads. It builds again before the final logo.
