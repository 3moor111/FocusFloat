**FocusFloat for Windows and Mac 1.0**: the floating timer, on your computer.

### What's in it
- **The floating timer**, always on top of your PDF, slides and browser. Click it for pause, quick capture, one break (3, 5 or 10 minutes) and end. At time's up it turns red: add +15m, +30m or +1h, or finish.
- **Quick capture**: save a flashcard, a question to look up after the session, or a note, while the timer keeps running.
- **Goal ring, streak and this week**, with the same rules as the phone: a session counts when it runs to the end and is at least 5 minutes long.
- **A report** after every session.
- **Flashcards** with spaced repetition, and the **Inbox**.
- Light and dark themes, and a check for new versions.

### Download
- **Windows 10 or 11:** `FocusFloat-1.0.0.msi`
- **Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and newer):** `FocusFloat-1.0.0-apple-silicon.dmg`
- **Mac with an Intel chip:** `FocusFloat-1.0.0-intel.dmg`

The app isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows, click **More info › Run anyway** the first time. On a Mac, **right-click › Open** the first time. [Full steps](https://3moor111.github.io/FocusFloat/install.html#desktop)
