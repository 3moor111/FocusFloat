**FocusFloat 3.8**, a focus app for students on Android.

A countdown floats over your PDF, lecture or browser. Around it: an app blocker, ScrollStop (hides Reels and Shorts), quick capture, flashcards, break quizzes and a report after every session.

### What's new in 3.8
- A new look: a Home card that changes with the time of day, an animated streak flame, a goal ring, and confetti at your daily goal
- Up next row, colourful tool tiles with live status, a Study card and This week ticks
- Bottom bar: Home, Progress, a big Focus button, Study, More
- Light, Dark or Like phone themes; the Inter typeface everywhere
- Close the floating timer by dragging it onto a bin
- Fixed two problems that stopped this version from building
