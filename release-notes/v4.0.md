**FocusFloat 4.0**: Reminders.

### What's new
- **Reminders**: alarms for anything you must not forget, medicine first. Once, every day, on chosen days, or every few hours, each with its own sound (the phone's alarm, a ringtone or your own audio file).
- It **rings over the lock screen** with **Done**, **Snooze** and **Skip**. Missed it? It rings again every 5 minutes, up to 6 times.
- An optional **what's left** counter, e.g. pills in the pack, that warns you when it runs low.
- A **Reminders** tile on Home, a timeline of today's reminders, and an editor with templates (Medicine, Appointment, Exam, Water, Study time) and a live preview.
- The next reminder also shows in Up next on Home.
