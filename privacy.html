Privacy

What FocusFloat sees, and what it keeps.

FocusFloat has no ads, no AI and no tracking. This page explains each kind of data in plain words.

Your account

Accounts use Firebase Authentication (Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase and never stored by FocusFloat. You can delete your account at any time in Profile; that removes your online data, your login and the profile’s data on that phone.

What is synced

Your name, email, daily goal, progress, session reports, cards, questions, Inbox notes and quiz answers are stored in Cloud Firestore under your account. The security rules allow only you to read or write them.

What stays on your phone

Block lists, schedules, ScrollStop settings, and which apps you used are never uploaded.

Usage access and notification access

Used only during a session, to see which app is on screen and to snooze notifications from blocked apps. Nothing about other apps is recorded or shared.

Accessibility (ScrollStop)

ScrollStop looks at the screen only inside Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Snapchat, to find the Reels, Shorts and Spotlight tabs and feeds. It covers those tabs and sends you back out of the endless feed. Nothing it sees is saved or sent anywhere. A screen scan is made only when you tap Scan in FocusFloat, records element names and positions (no pictures), and is shared only if you choose to send it.
