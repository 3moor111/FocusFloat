Updates

What’s new.

Every version of FocusFloat, newest first. To update, download the latest APK and install it over the one you have; your data stays.

4.0Latest
  • Reminders: alarms for anything you must not forget, medicine first. Once, daily, chosen days or every few hours, each with its own sound.
  • Rings over the lock screen with Done, Snooze and Skip, and rings again if you miss it.
  • An optional “what’s left” counter that warns you when a pack runs low.
  • A Reminders tile on Home, today’s timeline, and an editor with templates and a live preview.

Download 4.0

3.9
  • Updates inside the app: when a new version is out, Update available appears in Up next on Home. One tap downloads it, checks the file and opens the installer.
  • More › Check for updates checks any time and shows your version.
  • Downloads are only accepted from FocusFloat’s own releases, and must match the published fingerprint before they install.
  • Your progress, cards and settings stay when you update.
3.8
  • A new look: Home card that changes with the time of day (sunrise, sky blue, starry evening), animated streak flame, goal ring, confetti at your daily goal.
  • Up next row, colourful tool tiles with live status, a Study card and This week ticks.
  • Bottom bar: Home, Progress, a big Focus button, Study, More.
  • Light (default), Dark or Like phone themes. The Inter typeface everywhere, softer cards, indigo selections.
  • Close the floating timer by dragging it onto a bin.
  • Each tool’s screen opens with its own colour and art.
  • Fixed two problems that stopped this version from building.
3.7
  • Session in progress panel on Focus Timer and App Blocker: time left on a ring, end time, pause, break, extra time, blocking and exit level, End session.
  • ScrollStop page: its own screen instead of just closing the feed.
  • Google logo on Continue with Google.
3.6
  • Online accounts: sign up, log in, reset password, verify email, delete account. Continue with Google (3.6.1).
  • Sync of progress, reports, cards, questions, Inbox and quiz answers across devices. Old on-phone profiles move online on first login.
3.5
  • ScrollStop: hides Reels and Shorts, One Reel Pass, hours, exit levels, Scan this screen.
  • Card mode in quick capture, flashcards that flip and swipe, smoother motion everywhere.
  • Fix: the docked timer no longer sinks into the edge as the digits change.
3.4

  • Break quiz, practice mode, and the Second-guessing report.
3.3

3.1

3.0

2.0
